Know before you ride

  • Chrome or Edge only for sensors. Bluetooth works in Chrome and Edge on computers and in Chrome on Android. Safari, Firefox and every browser on iPhone and iPad can't connect sensors: there you can only run the demo.
  • You pick each sensor yourself. Tap SCAN on the sensors page and choose one in the browser's dialog, then again for the next. After a reload most browsers ask again.
  • Keep this page in front. A phone that locks or switches apps can pause the page, and with it the trainer's targets and the recording. The screen is kept on where the browser allows it.
  • Your data lives in this browser. Settings, workouts and rides are kept here, not on a server. Clearing site data removes them, so download the rides you want to keep (☰ → Download rides, or the share button in My rides).
  • Beta, and new. It has been ridden with a real trainer, but most models haven't been tried yet. If yours misbehaves, stop pedalling, close the page and tell us. The Android app is the better choice for a session that matters.

How to start

  1. Wake your trainer and sensors, and close other apps connected to them.
  2. Press Start, enter your FTP and weight, then open Settings → Sensors and tap SCAN for each one.
  3. Pick a workout or route. Ones made in the Workout Builder and the Route Builder can be dropped on the page or added with + ADD.
  4. ☰ (or M, or a long press) has the next step, harder and easier, finishing the ride and leaving.

Trainers with Bluetooth FTMS, and Tacx trainers on older firmware (FE-C over Bluetooth). Text is drawn in Liberation Sans (licence).
