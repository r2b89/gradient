Training that holds you to the number.

Pick a workout and pedal. Gradient tells the trainer what to do, step by step, and shows you exactly where you are in it.

Workouts in ERG

Hit every interval without touching a shifter.

The trainer holds each step's power whatever your cadence or gear. You see the step, its target, the time left, what comes next, and the whole session in zone colors.

  • 18 ready workouts: endurance, sweet spot, threshold 2×20, over-unders, VO2 max, 30/30s, Tabata, a ramp test and a 20-minute FTP test
  • Targets in % of FTP or in watts, with ramps and cadence goals
  • Harder or easier in 5 % steps at any time, or skip to the next step
Heart rate training

Honest Zone 2, without chasing your pulse.

Ride by heart rate and Gradient sets the power that brings your heart into the zone and keeps it there. Because a heart lags behind the effort, it shows the heart rate you should expect by now, so you don't overshoot trying to catch up.

  • Made for long aerobic rides and aerobic intervals
  • Set your FTP and max heart rate, and the zones follow
Real routes

Feel Alpe d'Huez in your legs.

The trainer gets every change of grade, and your speed comes from your power, your weight and the road. The profile ahead is colored by grade, with the distance and the climbing left.

  • Difficulty from 0 to 150 % of the real grades

Phones and tablets

Big numbers you can read mid-interval.

Power with its zone, cadence, speed, heart rate and the trainer's resistance, plus the route on a map and the last five minutes as graphs. On a tablet in landscape your ride's numbers stay beside every page. The screen stays on while you ride.

Your rides are yours

Standard FIT files, on your phone.

Every ride is saved as a FIT file, the format of Garmin's devices, every minute while you ride. Take it to any app that reads FIT, or open it in the free Rides page for graphs, zones and best efforts.

  • Strava
  • Garmin Connect
  • TrainingPeaks
  • intervals.icu

Works with the gear you have.

Standard Bluetooth profiles, so it isn't tied to one brand. Your sensors are remembered and connected again when you start.

Smart trainers

Any trainer with Bluetooth FTMS (Fitness Machine Service), which most current trainers support, and Tacx trainers on older firmware.

Heart rate monitors

Chest straps and armbands over Bluetooth, such as a Polar H10.

Power and cadence

Power meters and cadence or speed sensors. When two measure the same thing, you choose which one the app uses.
